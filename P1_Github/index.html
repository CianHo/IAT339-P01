Brand Identity

LatImports is a commercial food import company that brings food products from across Latin America into Canada for use in restaurants and grocery stores.

LatImports presents a fast, modern, and professional food import service that provides efficient service. The aesthetic of LatImports would be more corporate and modern rather than rustic.

LatImports could appeal to newer restaurants or chain establishments that would appreciate a somewhat impersonal supplier with quick service and no frills.

Possible Fonts

Montserrat

Montserrat provides a clean, modern sans-serif typeface that stems from urban signage in Buenos Aires. This ties into our both the intended attitude of LatImports (efficient and business-minded) as well as the region from which the products are sourced.

Gidole

Gidole is a simple sans-serif typeface that provides a sleek, almost futuristic look. This could be a potentially great match for the aesthetic of LatImports in a similar manner to Montserrat.

Qurova

Qurova leans even further into a futuristic look, which provides visual interest at the possible risk of being too busy or weird for the intended aesthetic.

Palette

For our palette we went with a primarily green palette supported by yellow and reds, with some blues. These colours are all common among many flags of Latin American countries. Green is also commonly associated with food. Using a cooler colour as the main identity helps maintain the professional feel of the company, but the vibrancy of the green and red combination prevents the palette from feeling stuffy. It was important to avoid having the colours feel too harsh, so the intensity of the colours was reduced overall.

Art Direction Inspiration

Our current direction takes inspiration from existing corporate food distributors such as Sysco and Gordon Food Service, which have simple and straightforward visual styles.
